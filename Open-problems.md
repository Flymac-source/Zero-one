# Open Problems

These are first-order open problems, not footnotes. Expanded from the living-document spine (coordination/consent pressure-tests plus technical and institutional gaps).

---

## Hard coordination and consent

1. **Coordination intensifies.** Planetary- or network-scale merger requires agreement on architecture, values, access, and governance among the same factions the project hopes not to be captured by.
2. **Voluntary under strong incentives is fragile.** Once deeper integration confers large cognitive, economic, and health advantages, holdouts face extreme pressure; voluntary vs effectively compulsory blurs. Education alone understates this.
3. **Shared infrastructure embeds whose values.** A universal mediation layer creates shared vulnerability and a high-value capture target. Decentralization, transparency, and autonomy constraints must be engineered and enforced against subversion.
4. **Interconnection ≠ peace.** Bandwidth does not guarantee empathy or shared objectives; conflict on a shared substrate can be more consequential.
5. **Identity continuity needs mechanisms.** “Evolve, not erase” is an assertion until mapping, coupling protocols, and legal continuity standards exist. Expansion-feel and loss-feel are both data.
6. **AI recursive improvement ≠ solved human coordination.** Closing self-improvement loops can amplify existing coordination or miscoordination.

**Strengthen-the-program checklist:** capture-prevention for interfaces and models; handling terminal-value disagreement under deep coupling; governance of the transition (powerful but not universal tech); exit options that remain credible after large-scale integration.

- Keeping exit rights real under strong network effects

- Handling terminal-value disagreement once deep coupling exists

## Closed-loop AI improvement (related, not sufficient)

If recursive improvement loops close (diagnose failures → propose changes → test → become next version) while remaining externally correctable, priority remains **reducing gap between outputs and reality**. Required constraints: objective = correspondence with reality (not engagement, approval, benchmarks-as-proxies, or self-preservation); corrigibility preserved; improvement process legible to outsiders. Without those, RSI is uncontrolled optimization.

## Related design notes (from the spine)

**Instrumental role of human minds (AI-side).** For narrow/medium-term goals, pure AI scaling is almost certainly more efficient (**N**). For this long-horizon collective, a carefully engineered human component could act as a high-bandwidth sensor and value oracle — not an equal partner by default. Possible advantages include embodied grounding, live preference data, exploratory diversity, and narrative continuity (**S** as merge claim; components partly **C**). Clear disadvantages: biological bottleneck, bias and short-horizon distortion, self-preservation instincts that can worsen corrigibility, opportunity cost vs pure architectural scaling.

**Continuity of persons (damaged or atypical minds).** Engineered middle path: (1) tiered participation with graded write influence; (2) contained simulations / private instances loosely coupled to the collective; (3) repair and enhancement first; (4) weighted / adversarially checked contribution. Simulations keep people continuous without forcing the collective to run at the reliability of its most impaired members. Abandonment and unfiltered integration are both rejected.
