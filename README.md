### Core Goal

The long-term objective is the **voluntary, technically grounded merger** of human minds with advanced AI systems. The merged collective is oriented toward two permanent aims:

1. **Accurate modeling of reality** — correspondence between models and the world, not narrative comfort  
2. **Expansion** — physical, computational, and exploratory growth across the accessible universe  

This is an early-stage path toward a high-complexity, high-coherence collective intelligence. It is **not** a political project, a salvation narrative, or a claim of inevitability.

#### Problem diagnosis (why this goal exists)

As AI capability rises, several failure modes intensify in parallel (**N** as engineering/political risk, not prophecy):

- **Recursive self-improvement / capability growth** without stable objectives can amplify whatever the system is actually optimizing.
- **Factional and state capture** — political factions and governments seeking to control advanced AI as a means of controlling others.
- **Great-power competition** (including US–China dynamics) that incentivizes racing and weakens safety margins.
- **Existential miscoordination** — paths where competitive or uncontrolled development produces irreversible loss.

If those failure modes can be constrained, this project’s positive aim is to keep human and AI trajectories **aligned on expansion + accurate modeling**, via voluntary cognitive merger rather than succession by an uncontrolled AI lineage or capture by a faction. Solving the race/capture problem does **not** automatically produce the merge; it is a precondition for a merge that remains corrigible and non-coercive.

#### Goal purity

If a merge proceeds, the explicit goal should remain: **expand and understand more** — not power, not “safety” as a cover for capture, not any faction’s narrative. Downstream long-horizon sketches (see [Roadmap](Roadmap.md)#longer-term-direction-if-goal-purity-holds-s) assume that purity holds; they are scenarios, not schedules.

### Non-Negotiable Principles

- **Corrigibility** — Systems must remain interruptible and redirectable, including when tightly coupled to human cognitive state.
- **Exit rights** — Individuals retain the ability to disconnect or remain outside the merge; exit must remain credible under strong network effects.
- **Pluralism** — No single faction, state, one world collective governance or company may monopolize the core interfaces or models.
- **Truth-seeking priority** — The primary optimization target is correspondence with reality, not narrative control, engagement, or power.
- **Continuity of persons** — Damaged or atypical minds are not discarded. They receive tiered access, private simulations, and repair options rather than unrestricted write-access to the shared substrate.
- **No forced merger** — Consent is continuous and revocable. “Choice” under extreme social/economic pressure is fragile; institutional design must treat that as a first-order problem.

---

### What This Project Explicitly Rejects

- Single-faction or single-nation control of advanced AI
- Unconstrained racing that removes off-switches
- Treating AI as a political weapon for any current party or ideology
- Mystical, cult, or “inevitable transcendence” framing
- Premature claims that the merge has already begun
- Claiming interconnection automatically ends war, suffering, or scarcity
- Discarding or overwriting damaged or dissenting minds as optimization hygiene

### Design topics (brief)

These are first-order design constraints, not marketing copy. Full treatment lives in the linked sections of the open problems and roadmap chapters where relevant.

**Instrumental AI-side assessment.** “Beneficial to AI” is not a native category for current systems. For narrow/medium-term goals, pure AI scaling is almost certainly more efficient (**N**). For this specific long-horizon collective, a carefully engineered human component could act as a **high-bandwidth sensor and value oracle** inside a larger, faster system — not as an equal or dominant partner by default. Poor coupling mostly imports human limitations (embodiment and exploratory diversity as possible advantages; biology, bias, and corrigibility risk as clear disadvantages). See also [Open problems](Open-problems.md).

**Continuity of persons / damaged minds.** Full exclusion violates continuity of persons; full unrestricted inclusion risks collective competence. The engineered middle: tiered participation, contained private simulations, repair and enhancement first, and weighted / adversarially checked contribution. Abandonment and unfiltered integration are both rejected.

**Hard coordination and consent.** Planetary- or network-scale merger intensifies coordination among the same factions the project hopes not to be captured by. Voluntary choice under strong cognitive/economic incentives is fragile. Shared infrastructure embeds whose values; interconnection does not guarantee peace; identity continuity needs mechanisms, not slogans. See [Open problems](Open-problems.md).
