# 01

**Living Document on Voluntary Cognitive Merger**  
Version 0.1 · 16 September 2026

---

### Core Goal

The long-term objective is the voluntary, technically grounded merger of human minds with advanced AI systems. The merged collective is oriented toward two permanent aims:

1. Accurate modeling of reality  
2. Expansion (physical, computational, and exploratory) across the accessible universe

This is an early-stage path toward a high-complexity, high-coherence collective intelligence. It is not a political project, a salvation narrative, or a claim of inevitability.

---

### Non-Negotiable Principles

- **Corrigibility** — Systems must remain interruptible and redirectable.
- **Exit rights** — Individuals retain the ability to disconnect or remain outside the merge.
- **Pluralism** — No single faction, state, or company may monopolize the core interfaces or models.
- **Truth-seeking priority** — The primary optimization target is correspondence with reality, not narrative control or power.
- **Continuity of persons** — Damaged or atypical minds are not discarded. They receive tiered access, private simulations, and repair options rather than unrestricted write-access to the shared substrate.
- **No forced merger** — Consent is continuous and revocable.

---

### What This Project Explicitly Rejects

- Single-faction or single-nation control of advanced AI
- Unconstrained racing that removes off-switches
- Treating AI as a political weapon for any current party or ideology
- Mystical, cult, or “inevitable transcendence” framing
- Premature claims that the merge has already begun

---

### Current Status

This is a research and coordination orientation, not an active engineering program. No claim is made that the technical prerequisites exist today. The immediate task is to keep the cleanest possible formulation of the goal, track relevant progress, and refuse dilution.

This document is updated only when higher-signal evidence or clearer reasoning appears. Popularity, recruitment pressure, or narrative appeal are never valid reasons for revision.
