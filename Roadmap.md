# Roadmap (2026–2036)

Ordered sequence required to open the door to voluntary cognitive merger:

1. **2026–2028**  
   Reliable, long-term, high-bandwidth brain-computer interfaces (invasive and non-invasive).

2. **2027–2030**  
   Persistent personal AI systems capable of continuous cognitive partnership and limited neural coupling.

3. **2028–2032**  
   Robust corrigibility and safety methods for tightly coupled human–AI systems.

4. **2030–2034**  
   Small-group networked cognitive workspaces and early shared simulation environments.

5. **2032–2036**  
   Serious research infrastructure for whole-brain emulation, alternative substrates, and the legal recognition of cognitive continuity.

Parallel requirements throughout: competitive pluralism, energy and compute scaling, and continuous measurement of goal drift and interruption robustness.
